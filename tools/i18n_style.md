# Translation style guide: madarekmatbaa.com (Arabic → English, Arabic → Turkish)

## Who we are and who reads it
Madarek Matbaa (مطبعة مدارك) is a B2B printing house and promotional-gifts supplier. Head office in Istanbul,
production in Damascus, sourcing in Turkey and China. Clients are companies, banks, insurers, telecoms,
government bodies and agencies. Every product is printed with the client's logo; prices are in USD;
quotes are requested on WhatsApp.

- **English site (/en/):** corporate buyers in the **Gulf (GCC: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman)**,
  plus Iraq and Libya. Procurement, marketing, HR and event teams. Write clear, confident, international business
  English (British spelling is fine but be consistent: use "colour", "organiser", "personalised"). Short sentences.
  No hype, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- **Turkish site (/tr/):** Turkish-speaking corporate buyers. Formal register (siz), natural Turkish B2B
  e-commerce wording, correct Turkish characters (ç ğ ı İ ö ş ü).

## Markers: keep them exactly
- `⟦1⟧ … ⟦/1⟧` wrap a link or bold text. Keep every pair, around the words that carry the same meaning.
  You may move a pair to where the words land in the translation. Never drop, add, merge or renumber them.
- `⟦2/⟧` is an icon, image or line break. Keep it, normally in the same position.
- `⟨0⟩`, `⟨1⟩` … stand for a product code, price, size or number. Keep every one exactly once; you may reorder.
  Example: `يبدأ السعر من ⟨0⟩` → `Prices start at ⟨0⟩`. `دفتر ⟨0⟩ ورقة` → `⟨0⟩-sheet notebook`.
- Keep `·`, `—`, `|`, `/` separators as in the source. Keep Latin words, URLs and e-mail addresses as they are.
- Output must contain no Arabic letters. Translate Arabic punctuation (، ؛ ؟) to Latin punctuation.

**Markets.** When Arabic lists where we ship (e.g. «سوريا وليبيا والعراق والخليج»): in English put the Gulf first —
"the GCC (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman), Iraq, Libya and Syria" in full sentences, or
"the Gulf, Iraq, Libya and Syria" when space is short (titles, meta descriptions). In Turkish keep the source order:
"Suriye, Libya, Irak ve Körfez ülkeleri". الخليج / دول الخليج = the Gulf / GCC countries = Körfez ülkeleri.

**SEO texts.** Page titles end with ` | Madarek Matbaa`. Keep titles under ~60 characters and descriptions under ~155
where the source allows. In English titles of category and product pages prefer buyer search wording:
"Custom Printed Ballpoint Pens with Logo", "Promotional Gifts", "Corporate Gifts".

## Units
cm, mm, ml, g (paper weight: gsm / g/m²), mAh, GB, W. سم → cm, مم → mm, مل → ml, غ → g (for paper: gsm),
مللي أمبير/ساعة → mAh, غيغابايت → GB. قطعة / قطع → piece(s) / adet. Dimensions stay `⟨0⟩ cm`.

## Product names
Type + distinguishing feature + size, in that natural order for the language. English: Title Case
("Bamboo Ballpoint Pen", "Wall Clock 400 mm"). Turkish: each main word capitalised ("Bambu Tükenmez Kalem").
Do not add words that are not in the source.
